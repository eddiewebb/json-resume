From the creators of Argo CD and Kargo, meet Akuity—the enterprise GitOps software delivery platform built for scale. Internal GitOps implementations often start strong with lightweight workflows and agile teams, but over time they become fragile, difficult to manage, and hard to scale across environments.

The Akuity Platform enables enterprise-grade GitOps at scale, supporting continuous multi-environment promotion, Kubernetes deployments, and unified visibility across all environments. With a single control plane, Akuity simplifies application delivery, environment promotion, and cluster management across thousands of clusters.

What you'll learn in this session:

* Simplify multi-cluster management across your org
* Unify promotions across Kubernetes, Terraform, VMs, and cloud services
* Reduce promotion complexity by eliminating custom scripts & tooling
* Reduce MTTR with automated incident resolution
* Real-world best practices for implementing GitOps at Scale
